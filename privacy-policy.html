Privacy Policy

AR Drawing: Sketch Cartoon - Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the AR Drawing: Sketch Cartoon app (package vn.ar.drawing, "the App"), published by vn.ar.drawing ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it.

1. Summary

The App has no accounts. Your camera view, your photos, your drawings and your progress stay on your device. The App is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob (an optional Premium subscription, sold through Google Play, removes them), and it uses Google Firebase Remote Config to receive app settings (for example which ads are switched on). Those two Google services receive the limited device data described in section 6. The App contains no analytics and no crash-reporting services.

2. Camera

The App asks for the camera permission so it can show the picture you are tracing on top of the live camera view. The camera image is processed on your device in real time. We do not record video, and the camera feed never leaves your device. You can refuse or withdraw the permission at any time in your device settings; the on-screen drawing mode keeps working without it.

3. Photos you choose or take

  • Pictures you pick to trace (through the system photo picker, or shared to the App from another app) are read only to display them while you trace. The App does not copy your photo library and receives only the picture you selected.
  • Photos of your drawing that you take with the camera button are saved in the App's private storage on your device ("My album"). They are not uploaded anywhere.

4. Information stored on your device

The App stores your preferences and progress on your device only: language, drawing level, favourite topics, daily goal, favourites, finished lessons, number of drawings and time spent drawing. This data and your album are deleted when you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.

5. Device backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the App's preferences in your device backup, under Google's own terms. Album photos are excluded from backup. We cannot read these backups.

6. Third-party services

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads (banners, full-screen ads and native ads) provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and interactions with ads. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalised. Google's use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and the ad partners are listed at AdMob ad technology providers.

Your choices: in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other regions where the law requires it, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the App. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's Google settings.

App configuration (Google Firebase Remote Config)

The App downloads its configuration from Firebase Remote Config. For this, Firebase receives a Firebase installation ID, your IP address and basic device and app information (such as app version, OS version and language). This data is used only to deliver the configuration. See the Firebase privacy information.

Premium subscription (Google Play Billing)

If you buy the optional Premium subscription, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details, name or email address. The App uses the Google Play Billing library to read whether your Google account has an active Premium subscription and stores a "Premium active" flag on your device, so ads stay off. Google's handling of purchase data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Bundled content

Bundled artwork comes from Openclipart (public domain) and fonts from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License); they are packaged inside the App and are not loaded from the internet.

Internet access

The App uses an internet connection only for the ads, configuration and Google Play subscription services above. It does not upload your photos, drawings or camera images.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; if you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
